## 2024-10-25 - [Accessibility Issue Pattern: Interactive Styles on Div Wrappers for Icon Buttons]
**Learning:** In this application, a common accessibility issue pattern is wrapping `<button>` elements inside non-semantic `<div>` wrappers and applying interactive styling (like `hover:bg-gray-700` and layout formatting) to the `<div>` instead of the `<button>`. This causes the interactive elements to lack proper focus styles and descriptive text (as they are often icon-only), making keyboard navigation difficult and breaking screen reader accessibility.
**Action:** When adding or auditing icon-only buttons, consistently refactor them by removing unnecessary `<div>` wrappers, applying interaction and layout styles directly to the `<button>`, ensuring descriptive `aria-label` attributes are present, and adding visible keyboard focus styles (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2`).
