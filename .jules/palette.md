## 2024-05-27 - Icon-only buttons wrapping pattern impacts keyboard accessibility
**Learning:** Found a specific pattern in this application's components (`Header.jsx`) where interactive styles (hover states, padding) and sometimes event handlers are placed on a parent `<div>` instead of the semantic `<button>` tag that wraps the icon. This configuration prevents native focus styles and accessible interactivity for keyboard/screen reader users, and also results in icon-only buttons lacking `aria-label`s.
**Action:** When working on icon-only buttons in this app, ensure interactive styles and handlers are placed directly on the `<button>` element. Always include `aria-label`s and explicit `focus-visible` ring styling to maintain accessibility along with existing design patterns.
