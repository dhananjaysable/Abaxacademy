## 2024-05-20 - Form Accessibility with Placeholders
**Learning:** UI components in this app often rely on placeholders without visible `<label>` elements.
**Action:** Ensure explicit `aria-label` attributes and focus visible styles (`focus:ring-2 focus:ring-blue-500 focus:outline-none`) are added to inputs for screen reader/keyboard accessibility to maintain existing design patterns while adhering to a11y standards.
